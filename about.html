The Daily Crumbs Manifesto

In a world of fast food and mass production, we choose the slow path. At Daily Crumbs, we believe that bread is a living thing. Our journey began in 2010 with a single sourdough starter—affectionately named "Eve"—which we still use today in every loaf we bake.

Our philosophy is simple: Patience, Passion, and Purity. We don't use commercial yeast, preservatives, or artificial enhancers. We use the ancient method of long-cold fermentation, allowing time to unlock the nutrients and flavors hidden deep within the grain.

The 48-Hour Artisan Process

Ever wonder why our bread tastes different? It's the luxury of time.

01. The Mix

We use stone-ground flour and filtered water, mixed gently to preserve the grain's natural integrity.

02. Fermentation

The dough rests for 24-36 hours in a temperature-controlled environment to develop its signature tang.

03. The Stone Bake

Finally, we bake at 250°C in stone-deck ovens to achieve that thick, caramelized "shatter" crust.
